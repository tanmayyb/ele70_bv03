  \begin{block}{Introduction}

  Energy forecasting and anomaly detection are critical components in the management of energy systems. Accurate energy forecasting allows utilities and organizations to predict energy demand and supply, enabling them to optimize resource allocation, reduce operational costs, and reduce carbon emissions.

  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Problem Statement}
    In this project, our goal is to predict energy demand and detect anomalies in energy consumption habits of zones and cities in Ontario using advanced time series forecasting and anomaly detection methods to allow utilities and organizations to make informed decisions.
  \end{block}
